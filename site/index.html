Built for people who fill disks fast.

If you write code, node_modules, Xcode DerivedData, build folders, simulators and SDK caches quietly eat tens of gigabytes. Crufto Cleaner finds them first — then helps everyone else reclaim the caches and leftovers macOS never cleans up.

What Crufto Cleaner clears

See where it all went

A fast scan breaks your storage down by category — including the hidden System Data and purgeable space that Finder won't show you.

Developer clutter

node_modules, DerivedData, build directories, old simulators and SDK caches — the gigabytes your tools leave behind.

Caches & app leftovers

Stale caches, logs, and the files left scattered across your system by apps you deleted long ago.

Reversible by default

Nothing is shredded. Everything moves to the Trash, and a History view puts any item back exactly where it was.

Three tiers of safety

Items are sorted into safe to remove, review first, and manual — so you decide, and the risky stuff is never swept up by accident.

Memory monitor

A live view of memory pressure and the background processes using it, so you can see what's slowing things down.

Safe by design

  • Reversible. Removals go to the Trash with one-click Put Back — not an irreversible delete.
  • It asks before it acts. You review what's selected; the three-tier system keeps anything uncertain out of the automatic pile.
  • Private. Crufto Cleaner scans on your Mac and never uploads your files anywhere.
  • Notarized. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so Gatekeeper opens it cleanly.
